Quick start

  • Edit prices, copy and delivery files in the Catalogue tab. That is what the storefront sells; the Courses and Products tabs are the legacy table and no longer drive the site.
  • Orders paid over WhatsApp arrive as Submitted with the buyer's payment reference. Check it, then press Confirm in the Orders tab — that sends the receipt and unlocks the download.
  • Inspect the audit trail in the Transactions tab.
