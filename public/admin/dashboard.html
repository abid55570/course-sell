Overview

Manage courses, orders, and support.

Quick start

  • Add a new course from the Courses tab. Toggle whether each course's PDF, Drive link, or custom email template is sent on payment confirmation.
  • Verify UPI payments and confirm orders in the Orders tab. The customer gets the access email automatically.
  • Inspect the audit trail in the Transactions tab.
