Catalogue

Everything the storefront sells.

Edit a product to change its price, copy, delivery file, or whether it is live. Saved changes appear on the site within a few seconds.

Orders paid over WhatsApp arrive as Submitted with the buyer's payment reference. Check the reference against the chat, then press Confirm — that sends the receipt email and unlocks the download. Once Razorpay is live, orders complete automatically.

How this store runs

  • Change prices, copy and delivery files in Catalogue — that is what the storefront sells.
  • Confirm WhatsApp payments in Orders; the buyer's receipt and download go out automatically on Confirm.
  • Review every order event in Transactions.
  • The Advanced tabs are older product lines the storefront no longer reads — leave them unless you sell tools, the carousel editor, or invite videos.
