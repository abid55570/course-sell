Questions

Is there a per-certificate charge?

No. Pay ₹149 once and generate as many certificates as you like — 50 for a webinar or 5,000 for a fest, same price.

Can I preview before paying?

Yes. Build and preview for free. The free tier exports the first 3 certificates with a watermark so you can check the result. Unlock to remove the cap and watermark.

What does my CSV need?

A column named "name". You can also add "course" and "date" columns to personalise each certificate. No headers? Just paste one name per line instead.

How do I get the files?

Two ways: a single combined PDF (one landscape A4 page per person), or a ZIP with a separate PDF for each recipient named by their name.

Is it a subscription?

No — it's a one-time ₹149 payment. You get a license key that unlocks the tool forever. Lost it? Recover it by email in the editor.
