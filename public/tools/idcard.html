Questions

Do I need to pay before I see it?

No. Pick a template, upload your CSV and preview for free. The free tier exports up to 3 cards with a watermark; pay ₹149 once for unlimited, watermark-free cards.

What columns should my CSV have?

Column headers should match the card field keys — for a school card that's name, id, class, blood. You can also add a `photo` column with an image URL for each person.

Is it a subscription?

No — it's a one-time ₹149 payment. You get a license key that unlocks the tool forever.

What format do I get?

A print-ready A4 PDF with cards arranged in a grid, plus the option to download any single card as a high-resolution PNG.

Lost my license key?

Recover it by email right inside the editor — click Unlock, then "Lost your key?".
